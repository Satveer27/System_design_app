# FPL Analytics

A free site where Fantasy Premier League managers see predicted points for every player, plan their team for the next gameweek and check how well the predictions did.

## The game

**Gameweek**:
One round of Premier League fixtures in the FPL season, numbered 1 to 38.
_Avoid_: Week, round, matchday

**Deadline**:
The time after which managers can no longer change their team for a gameweek.
_Avoid_: Cutoff, lock time

**Fixture**:
One Premier League match between two clubs in a gameweek.
_Avoid_: Game, match

**Club**:
A Premier League football club.
_Avoid_: Team

**Player**:
A footballer who can be picked in FPL, with a position, a club and a price.
_Avoid_: Footballer, element

**Manager**:
A person who plays FPL, identified by their FPL team ID.
_Avoid_: FPL user, entry

**Squad**:
The 15 players a manager owns, within the budget and at most three per club.
_Avoid_: Team, roster

## This site

**User**:
A person with an account on this site, who may link a manager's team ID.
_Avoid_: Account, member

**Prediction**:
The expected points for one player in one gameweek, fixed before the deadline.
_Avoid_: Projection, forecast, xP

**Baseline**:
FPL's own expected points for a player, used as the yardstick a prediction must beat.
_Avoid_: Benchmark, ep_next

**Plan**:
A user's saved squad for one gameweek, with starting 11 and captain, used to compare predicted scores before making changes in FPL.
_Avoid_: Draft, lineup, team

**Best Squad**:
The squad with the highest total prediction that FPL's rules allow in a gameweek.
_Avoid_: Optimal team, dream team, model team

**Tier List**:
A user's ranking of players into named tiers, which can be shared.
_Avoid_: Ranking, board

**Scorecard**:
The public comparison of predictions and the baseline with what players actually scored in a gameweek.
_Avoid_: Report, accuracy page, leaderboard

**Favourite**:
A player or club a user has bookmarked to follow.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, watchlist, follow
